Shop Bot

A Telegram shopping bot for digital goods — browse the catalog, pay your way, get your items delivered automatically.

What it does

The bot sells digital products straight inside Telegram: account logins, license keys, game top-ups and downloadable files. No website, no app install — the whole shop lives in a chat.

Digital accountsLicense keysGame top-upsFiles

How it works

  • 1Browse the catalog — products are organized in categories; new arrivals wait for approval before they go visible.
  • 2Pay your way — Binance, bKash, or MEXC deposit. Send the payment, then submit your transaction ID in the bot.
  • 3Get it instantly — once confirmed, the bot auto-delivers your digital goods right in the chat.
